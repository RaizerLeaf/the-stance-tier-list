Score formula
Score = 100 × [1 − (1 − P)2.8], where P is the build's performance relative to a reference flagship (RTX 5090 + Ryzen 9 9950X3D on AM5 = 1.0). P multiplies: GPU relative performance (TechPowerUp hierarchy, RTX 5090 = 100%) · features by upscaling generation (DLSS 4 Multi Frame Gen 1.04, DLSS Frame Gen 1.02, machine-learned upscaling 1.00, shader FSR only 0.98, no ML upscaling and no hardware ray tracing 0.92) · VRAM (−2.5% per GB under 12 GB; shared console pools count at 75%) · CPU as √(gaming index ÷ 100), where 100 is a 9950X3D · platform (AM5 1.02, dead Intel sockets 0.99) · system RAM (under 32 GB 0.97) · legacy (platforms no longer receiving current releases 0.80). The curve flattens toward the top, so flagships stay hard to separate and S stays hard to reach, and is near-linear through the console and mid-range zone. Near-ties go to the newer architecture through the features and platform multipliers. Fixed platforms that cross-platform testing shows at parity, PS5 and Series X, share one effective GPU and CPU rating, and consoles and handhelds are rated on their TV or docked output. In the VRAM column, shared means one memory pool serves both CPU and GPU, so the figure is not directly comparable to a dedicated card.

The AI Scaling Revolution: DLSS vs FSR

While raw rasterization defines traditional graphics pipelines, contemporary software leverages temporal upscalers to completely transform game performance.

NVIDIA DLSS (Deep Learning Super Sampling)
  • Hardware-Accelerated ML: Dedicated Tensor Cores execute local deep learning models, reconstructing crystal-clear frames with virtually zero ghosting artifacts.
  • Frame Generation (DLSS 3): Exclusive to RTX 40 and 50 series, injecting entire AI-generated frames to double framerates.
  • Ray Reconstruction (DLSS 3.5/4): Extends temporal stabilization and maximizes lighting accuracy in real-time ray-traced engines.
AMD FSR (FidelityFX Super Resolution)
  • The RX 9000 & FSR 4 Shift: FSR 4 introduces fully machine-learned temporal upscaling, departing from the hand-crafted hand-tuned heuristics of FSR 3.
  • AI Core Accelerators: RX 9000 uses dedicated AI accelerator blocks in the compute units to execute FSR 4's neural networks with near-zero latency.
  • FSR 4 vs FSR 3 Breakthrough: Despite having a smaller 192-bit bus and 16GB VRAM compared to the legacy RX 7900 XTX's 384-bit bus and 24GB VRAM, the Radeon RX 9070 XT comfortably outperforms the RX 7900 XTX in modern gaming when utilizing ML-driven FSR 4. The 7900 XTX is limited to shader-bound FSR 3 algorithms, resulting in higher rendering overhead and softer image outputs.
